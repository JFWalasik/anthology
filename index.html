          Backups

          A backup is one file with all your notes, task lists and folders. Restoring one replaces everything in Anthology; what it replaces goes to Recently deleted for 30 days.

          Help and About

          Anthology is a notes and tasks app, built to be simple and to last. The Notes/Tasks switch at the top moves between your notes and your task lists; folders are shared by both.

          Adding Anthology to your home screen

          iPhone: open Anthology in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up), then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then Add to Home screen or Install app.

          Getting in on a new device

          Enter your email, then the 6-digit code emailed to you. On iPhone, enter the code in the home-screen app itself, since it signs in separately from Safari.

          Syncing and working offline

          Changes are saved on your device straight away and sent to your other devices when there's a connection. Without one, keep working; changes go up when you're back online. The bottom of the sidebar shows the sync status.

          Recently deleted

          Deleted notes, task lists, folders and tasks can be restored for 30 days from Recently deleted, at the bottom of the sidebar.

          Sharing

          Open a note or task list and choose Share to get a link. Anyone with the link can see it, without signing in, and sees your changes as you make them. Turn links off under Shared links.
